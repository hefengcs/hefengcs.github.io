hefengcs [at] mail.ustc [dot] edu [dot] cn

I received my M.S. from the University of Science and Technology of China in June 2026. My research focuses on reliable reasoning and agentic behavior in large language models, particularly how models make decisions under unreliable premises, feedback, and verification signals. I am especially interested in reliable multi-step and multi-agent systems.

I have collaborated with Prof. Jieyu Zhao, Prof. Dongfang Liu, and Prof. Ruixiang Tang on topics spanning multi-agent systems, LLM reasoning, and trustworthy AI. Previously, I worked on scientific foundation models and multimodal representation learning at USTC.

I am applying for Fall 2027 PhD programs in reliable agentic AI and LLM reasoning.
