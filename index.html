Research Questions

I believe intelligence emerges from a deep understanding of problems and the development of scalable methods to solve them. My research focuses on two aspects.

1. Understanding & Diagnosing: How do intelligent systems represent information, reason over it, and where and why do they fail?

  • Structured Representations for scientific imaging and physical consistency (XLFM-Former, NeurIPS 2025)
  • Hallucinations and their impact on text-based and multimodal reasoning (HIVE, ECCV 2026; PHRBench, Under Review)
  • Verification Failures under false premises (Verification Trap, EMNLP 2026 Oral)
  • Reasoning Vulnerability under task specifications (PSR, Under Review)

2. Learning from Trajectories: How can intelligent systems learn from their experiences to become more capable and reliable?

  • Scalable Oversight through learning from failure and intervention trajectories (OversightHarness)
  • Continual Adaptation through deliberate reasoning and rapid learning from experience (Slow-Fast Evolving)
  • Knowledge Abstraction from execution trajectories for future decision making (Latent Learning Harness)
  • Harness Learning with smaller models that construct and optimize agent harnesses (Small Models as Harness Learners)
